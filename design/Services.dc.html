Maize and bean planting

Planting support uses available planting equipment, including hand‑held planters. The team discusses field readiness, crop requirements and the appropriate equipment for the task. Seed, fertiliser and other input arrangements are agreed separately.

Tell us: your crop, field location, area in hectares and preferred planting period.

Maize shelling and rice threshing

These services separate maize grain from the cob or rice grain from the harvested crop. Requests are assessed against crop readiness, machine suitability and operator availability. We agree the working area, handling arrangements and any transport charges before scheduling.

Tell us: crop, estimated quantity in kilograms or tonnes, location and preferred date.

Training and agricultural advisory support

Our practical learning activities cover machinery use, crop establishment, soil health, spraying practices, post‑harvest handling and farm‑business skills. Farmers and groups can enquire about training or advisory support for a specific need.

Tell us: the topic or skill you need support with, and your group size.

Machinery operation and maintenance learning

We support operators and farmer groups with practical learning on equipment operation, routine checks, cleaning and maintenance.

Tell us: the machine type and the support needed.
