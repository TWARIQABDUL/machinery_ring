About Machinery Ring Rwanda

We work with local farmer groups to coordinate service needs, develop practical skills and strengthen the organisation required to manage agricultural activities.

Who we are

Machinery Ring Rwanda supports farmers in accessing agricultural machinery and related services through cooperation. We work with local farmer groups to coordinate service needs, develop practical skills and strengthen the organisation required to manage agricultural activities.

What is a Machinery Ring

A Machinery Ring (MR) is a local, farmer‑led self‑help organisation that helps members access agricultural machinery and related services through cooperation. Farmers who own machinery can offer services to other members at agreed rates that cover the cost of providing them. A farmer may provide one service while hiring another from a fellow member. This approach improves equipment use and gives farmers access to mechanisation without each farmer having to purchase their own machinery.

Our development in Rwanda

Implementation in Rwanda began in 2024 through a project supporting the Machinery Ring approach in Kenya and Rwanda. Activities have included establishing local Rings, introducing equipment, organising farmer training and developing demonstration farms. Machinery Ring Rwanda obtained legal recognition in 2025.

Our purpose is to strengthen farmers’ access to suitable machinery, practical knowledge and coordinated services. Local participation helps shape which services are needed and how they can be organised.

Joining a Machinery Ring

Contact our team or a local Ring representative to learn about membership, the applicable conditions and available services. Farmers can also coordinate requests with neighbours so that work and machinery movements can be planned together. Non‑members may enquire about services and the terms that apply to them.
