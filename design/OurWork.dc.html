Our Work

Our work combines farmer organisation, machinery access and practical learning. Local Rings help bring requests together, while training and field activities support the skills needed to use and manage equipment.

The Kenya and Rwanda mechanisation project

The project “Sustainable mechanization of value chains through innovative, gender‑sensitive business models — Machinery Rings in Kenya and Rwanda” runs from November 2023 to October 2026. Work in Rwanda began in 2024, focusing on local Rings, agricultural services, training and demonstration activities in Gashora.

Implemented by the Machinery Ring Foundation, with support from GIZ Rwanda and sequa gGmbH under German Development Cooperation.

Demonstration farms and practical learning

Demonstration farms give farmers opportunities to examine equipment and farming practices under local conditions. Activities connect seasonal crop management with machinery suitability, soil management and post‑harvest learning. Field experience also helps identify where equipment or service arrangements need improvement.

Photo — Participants listen to a practical explanation beside agricultural processing equipment.
Women’s participation

We support women’s participation through savings‑group activities, financial literacy, leadership and digital‑skills training. This work includes group management, savings and loan practices, and links with local financial institutions. These activities help women take part in the organisational and business work of their farmer groups.
